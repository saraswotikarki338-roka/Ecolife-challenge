Daily Eco Challenges

Complete these simple eco-friendly challenges and make a positive impact on the environment.

♻️ Recycle Waste

Separate paper, plastic and glass into recycling bins.

💧 Save Water

Reduce your shower time by at least 2 minutes.

⚡ Save Energy

Switch off lights and electrical devices when not in use.

🌳 Plant a Tree

Plant a tree or care for an existing plant today.

🛍️ Reusable Bags

Use reusable shopping bags instead of plastic bags.

🚶 Walk More

Walk or cycle instead of using a car for short trips.

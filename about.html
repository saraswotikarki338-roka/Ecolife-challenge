About EcoLife Challenge

EcoLife Challenge is an educational website designed to encourage people to develop eco-friendly habits through daily challenges, learning resources, quizzes and a carbon footprint calculator.

Our Mission

Our mission is to inspire individuals to make small changes in their daily lives that contribute to protecting the environment and creating a sustainable future.

Our Goals

  • Promote environmental awareness.
  • Encourage sustainable lifestyles.
  • Educate users about climate change.
  • Help users reduce their carbon footprint.
